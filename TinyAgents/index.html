Tiny Agents

Every active Claude Code session (and each subagent it spawns) shows up as a tiny coworker who drives in, parks, and takes the elevator up. Busy agents sit at their desk and type; when Claude is waiting on you they take breaks, grab coffee, and chat.

Look around

  • Drag or WASD to pan; Scroll, pinch or +/− to zoom
  • Click an agent to see what they're up to and change their look
  • Clock follows your local time (night falls outside), Day keeps it sunny
  • The weather button next to it shows sunny or rain: showers come and go by themselves, and a click switches between the two

Edit the office

  • E toggles edit mode
  • Assets: furniture, decor and outdoor pieces · Select: move, rotate (R), swap for a look-alike, Del
  • Right-drag or Space+drag pans while editing

Connect Claude

  1. Tap the status pill at the top → Create my office
  2. Add a computer: paste the setup prompt into Claude Code on each computer, once
  3. Add a device: join from your phone or other browsers with a code

Click a Claude agent to follow what it's doing and read the conversation. Reply in VS Code, or from your phone with Remote Control in the Claude app.
